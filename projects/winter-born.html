Winter-Born

In a world devastated by a mysterious infection, survival comes at a cost. For Hazel, a young woman carrying a secret within her own blood, the greatest threat may not be the creatures lurking beyond the safety of civilization, but the people determined to control what she has become.

Writing samples

Chapter One

The moon revealed its broken pieces through shards of glass scattered across the floor. The wood creaked under the weight of Hazel's boot. She stopped for a moment to glance through the window. The world was still, too still.

Her eyes drifted toward her right arm. Just past her wrist, an oily black liquid seeped through her veins. Hazel felt her eyes grow heavy. She turned to lean over the counter. Her shaking hands were coated in a bruised, ultraviolet liquid. Still, they could not hide the blackening veins making their way up her arm.

Closing her eyes she let out a long shaky breath. She grabbed a small, dull, kitchen knife that lay on the counter. Turning around to face a man, Alvarez, tied to a chair. For a second they locked eyes.

His face was swollen. His outfit covered in dried blood. Still, Hazel could see his eyes drift toward her arm. There was no point in hiding it now.

She moved over to him. The moonlight caught the knife, showing off its rough edges. Blood leaking from the knife's tip. She was no longer hiding what lay beneath her pale skin.

Excerpt from an ongoing manuscript. Currently undergoing developmental revision.

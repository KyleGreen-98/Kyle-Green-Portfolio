ORIGINAL SCREENPLAY / ROAD DRAMA

Route 66

Where the Lost Get Found.

An original feature screenplay following George and Sarah on a journey across America, beginning in Chicago. Through unexpected encounters and changing landscapes, the story explores connection, distance, and the possibility of finding something you didn't know you were looking for.

Route 66 — Screenplay Excerpt

INT. HOTEL ROOM 306 – MOMENTS LATER

George opens his room's door. His silhouette visible from the doorway. Heavy silence.

A beat.

He takes a step inside and we cut to—

INT. HOTEL ROOM 307

Sarah opens her hotel room immediately flicking the lights ON. She strolls inside and places her suitcase against the wall. She carefully places her notebook and Polaroid on the desk.

We notice the Polaroid says: PROPERTY OF NATHAN BURROWS. Sarah traces a finger along the words.

A beat.

INTERCUT

George and Sarah both sit on their respective beds. George is exhausted, rubbing his forehead. Looks over at his suitcase.

A beat. He walks over to it.

Sarah is the opposite, taking in every detail of the room. She goes to sit at the desk.

George turns on a lamp light. He's looking at his father's picture. Sarah is writing again.

INT. ROOM 306 – MORNING

The window curtains flow with the breeze, a soft light leaking through. George's silhouette can be seen sitting on the bed. It's unclear if he slept.

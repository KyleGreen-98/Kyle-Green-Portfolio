02 / ABOUT

The person behind
the stories.

I'm Kyle Green, an independent writer based in Wisconsin, exploring storytelling through original fiction, screenwriting, and interactive narrative concepts. I'm especially drawn to character-driven experiences, psychological horror, worldbuilding, and environmental storytelling.

My current work includes The Cornerstone, a psychological horror game concept in narrative development, and Winter-Born, a supernatural horror novel with a completed initial draft under revision.

I'm interested in remote junior narrative writing, narrative design support, and creative collaboration opportunities. I do not currently have professionally shipped game credits.
